This table is for explaining a result after the fact: with the same ballots, another rule can pick another winner. The rule has to be fixed before the vote and must not be chosen after seeing results. The agreed rule is Condorcet (Schulze completion) and its winner is 爬山.

## Not modeled

- No protection against strategic voting: with three or more options, every ranking rule that can elect each option and is not a dictatorship can be manipulated by some voter in some situation (the Gibbard-Satterthwaite theorem).
- A ballot cannot rank two options equally.
- The script does not check who may vote, whether someone voted twice or whether a ballot is genuine. The organizer does.
