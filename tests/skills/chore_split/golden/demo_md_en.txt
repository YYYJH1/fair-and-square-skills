## Round-by-round check for week 1 (everyone against everyone, using their own ranking)

| Who | Against | Order | Round by round | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 阿明 | 小红 | picks before them | round 1 倒垃圾(1) <= their same round 洗碗(5); round 2 做饭(2) <= their same round 擦窗(4) | no envy |
| 阿明 | 大刘 | picks before them | round 1 倒垃圾(1) <= their same round 拖地(3); round 2 做饭(2) <= their same round 刷厕所(6) | no envy |
| 小红 | 阿明 | picks after them | round 1 洗碗(2) <= their round 2 做饭(6) | no envy once the last pick "擦窗" is set aside |
| 小红 | 大刘 | picks before them | round 1 洗碗(2) <= their same round 拖地(4); round 2 擦窗(3) <= their same round 刷厕所(5) | no envy |
| 大刘 | 阿明 | picks after them | round 1 拖地(1) <= their round 2 做饭(3) | no envy once the last pick "刷厕所" is set aside |
| 大刘 | 小红 | picks after them | round 1 拖地(1) <= their round 2 擦窗(6) | comparing the whole bundles: no envy |

## Random source and how to recompute

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Where the source comes from | 虚构示例：2026 年 11 月 8 日某公开开奖，按官网显示的格式原样抄写 |
| Source text (verbatim) | （虚构示例）2026-11-08 某公开开奖号码 03 11 19 24 33 + 02 07 |
| Rule name | chore_pick_order_v1 |
| Chore units per week (part of the seed, the n_slots of the seed) | 6 |
| Names (sorted by code point, part of the seed) | 大刘, 小红, 阿明 |
| Seed preimage (take the SHA-256 of this text) | {"n_slots":6,"participants":["大刘","小红","阿明"],"rule":"chore_pick_order_v1","source":"（虚构示例）2026-11-08 某公开开奖号码 03 11 19 24 33 + 02 07"} |
| Seed (SHA-256, hex) | 6944c304e73fa353e0d827408cc1f48fd3a8684494793fc65a25fa07a39f46a1 |
| Pick order of week 1 | 阿明, 小红, 大刘 |

The number in brackets is the place of the chore in that person's own ranking (1 is the least disliked). <= means no worse than the other person's pick. A person who picks before the other is compared with the same round, a person who picks after the other with the next round, because the other has already taken a chore in the current one.

EF1 status (nobody envies anybody once one chore is set aside): holds. The script checked every pair of people in all 1 week. All 9 round-by-round comparisons hold.

Rotation: the pick order of week 1 comes from the source, then the start moves one place each week. After 3 weeks everybody has started once.

How to recompute the pick order of week 1: (1) seed = SHA-256 of the seed preimage (UTF-8). (2) Draw number c (counting from 0) = the first 8 bytes, big-endian, of SHA-256(seed + c as 8 big-endian bytes), read as an integer x. For an integer in 0..n-1, take x mod n if x < 2^64 - (2^64 mod n), otherwise add 1 to c and draw again. (3) Sort the names by code point, go i from n-1 down to 1, draw an integer j in 0..i and swap positions i and j. The result is the pick order of week 1. The random module is never used.

One line per person (send it to that person only, not to the group):
- 阿明: In every round, the chore you picked (round 1 倒垃圾, round 2 做饭) is no worse in your ranking than the one each other person picked right after you: the same round if they pick after you, their next round if they pick before you. At most one chore is an exception: set it aside and you envy nobody.
- 小红: In every round, the chore you picked (round 1 洗碗, round 2 擦窗) is no worse in your ranking than the one each other person picked right after you: the same round if they pick after you, their next round if they pick before you. At most one chore is an exception: set it aside and you envy nobody.
- 大刘: In every round, the chore you picked (round 1 拖地, round 2 刷厕所) is no worse in your ranking than the one each other person picked right after you: the same round if they pick after you, their next round if they pick before you. At most one chore is an exception: set it aside and you envy nobody.

## Not modeled

- Whether the source was really fixed in advance, cannot be changed afterwards and could not be predicted: the script cannot check this. It only decides who picks first in week 1.
- Whether the rankings were entered faithfully: each person checks theirs in their own message. How much a chore is disliked beyond the order, for example if somebody finds dishes ten times worse than mopping, is not seen by the script.
- Strategy-proofness: round-robin picking is not strategy-proof. A person who knows the other rankings can sometimes get a lighter set by ranking untruthfully.
- Across weeks: every week is guaranteed to be EF1 on its own. Nothing is promised about the weeks added together.
- Tasks that somebody likes: not supported. A liked task declared in likes is refused.
- Paying somebody to take a chore: this plan moves no money. To compensate housemates with money, set rules.plan to bundle_pay. What an outside cleaner costs is not calculated. Use bill-split to share that.
