Chore split (bundles with compensation): 4 people, 10 chores, smallest weekly total cost
Rules (fixed in advance, not changed after the result): everybody has a cap on the number of chores, and nobody is given a chore they cannot do. Within those limits the split with the smallest weekly total cost is taken. Then payments that add up to zero make sure that nobody, by their own costs, would rather have another person's share.
Tie rule: when more than one split has the smallest total cost, the chores are given out one unit at a time in the order of their names. Each goes to the person who comes first in the tie order below and for whom a split with the smallest total cost is still possible.
